# MemeEditor Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-30  
**App:** MemeEditor (`com.memeeditor.app`)

## Summary

MemeEditor is a local meme creator. Photos and memes you create stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them using the system share sheet or your photo library.

## Data we collect

We do **not** collect personal information, analytics, advertising IDs, or crash reports. The app does not require an account and does not contact our servers.

## Photos and media

- **Templates** ship with the app.
- **Custom images** you pick from your gallery are copied into app cache only to edit the meme. They are not uploaded.
- **Save to gallery** writes the finished JPEG to your photo library (with your permission on platforms that require it).
- **Share** hands the finished JPEG to the system share sheet; the destination app is under your control.

## Permissions

- **Photo library (add / write):** used only when you tap Save to gallery.
- **Photo picker:** used only when you pick a custom background; the system picker does not grant broad library access.

## Children

MemeEditor does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the developer at the support email listed on the Google Play / App Store listing.

## Changes

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
