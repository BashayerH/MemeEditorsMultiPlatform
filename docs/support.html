App: MemeEditor (com.memeeditor.app)

Help

  • Pick a template or your own photo, add text, then share or save to your gallery.
  • If saving to Photos fails on iPhone, open Settings → MemeEditor → Photos and allow access.
  • Include your device model and OS version when reporting a problem.
